# Assets and licences

Every third-party asset in the app or repo is listed here with its source and licence. Original art (the pixel sprites in `packages/sprites`, drawn as code) is not listed.

| Asset | Used for | Source | Licence |

| Pixelify Sans (weights 500, 700) | Headings, numbers, rewards | `@expo-google-fonts/pixelify-sans` (Google Fonts) | SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 |
| Inter (weights 400, 500, 600) | Body text | `@expo-google-fonts/inter` (Google Fonts) | SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 |
| Placeholder app icon and splash images (`apps/mobile/assets/*.png`) | Temporary icon until P6 | Expo `blank-typescript` template, SDK 58 | MIT, Copyright (c) 2015-present 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo) |

Fonts are added to the app in P1. The placeholder icons are replaced with original art in P6. No other third-party assets are used (see DECISIONS.md D1, answer 4).
